### Giorgi Metskhvarishvili
**Engineering Leader · Software & Solution Architect** · .NET, cloud-native & distributed systems · Tbilisi, Georgia

Engineering leader and software architect with 10+ years of experience building large-scale distributed systems in gaming, banking, government and iGaming. I architected a mobile esports platform from scratch to 40+ microservices, 2.6M+ users and 99.98% availability, broke a bank's vendor monolith into in-house microservices handling 1–1.5M transactions a day, and led a ~60-person government IT organization through the modernization of 26+ legacy applications. Hands-on in C#/.NET and cloud-native infrastructure, with deep expertise in DDD, CQRS, event-driven architecture, payments, security and fraud prevention.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gmetskhvarishvili/) · [NuGet](https://www.nuget.org/profiles/gmetskhvarishvili) · [GitHub](https://github.com/gmetskhvarishvili)

#### Technical expertise

- **Architecture:** Distributed Systems, Microservices, Event-Driven Design, Domain-Driven Design, CQRS, Clean Architecture, Event Storming
- **Languages:** C#, Java, Kotlin, Swift, JavaScript
- **Frameworks:** .NET, ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework, FluentValidation, Polly, Wolverine, SignalR, Quartz, Angular, React, Android SDK
- **Data & Messaging:** PostgreSQL, MS SQL, Oracle, Redis, Valkey, Elasticsearch, RabbitMQ, Apache Kafka
- **Cloud & DevOps:** AWS (EKS, RDS, ElastiCache), Microsoft Azure, DigitalOcean, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Argo CD, Bitbucket Pipelines, HashiCorp Vault, Doppler
- **Observability:** Datadog, Dynatrace, Elastic APM, Grafana, Graylog, Cisco AppDynamics

#### Experience

- **Lead Software Architect**, MobileESports (part of BoomBit) (Dec 2022 – present). Architected the platform from scratch and launched it in 8 months: 40+ microservices, 2.6M+ users, 3.4M+ payout transactions, ~2,900 automated tournaments a day, 99.98% availability and ~50 production deployments a week. Built the fraud-prevention system (43,000+ accounts blocked or suspended).
- **Software Engineering Team Lead**, Betsson Georgia (Mar 2026 – Jun 2026). Led ~15 engineers across five product teams. Migrated Sports services to AWS (EKS, RDS, ElastiCache, Amazon MQ), cutting their infrastructure costs by 30% and making their API responses 35% faster; archived 300+ repositories and decommissioned 50+ obsolete services.
- **Associate Partner, Software Development Chapter Lead**, TBC Bank (Apr 2023 – Mar 2026). Led the Backend Chapter of 10–20 engineers across 10+ product teams and recruited 20+ engineers. Broke the vendor's monolith into ~15 in-house asynchronous microservices and personally built the Transfers service, handling 1–1.5M transactions a day.
- **Software Solutions Architect**, Kanti (May 2024 – Oct 2025). Designed and single-handedly built the backend of a raffle platform for the 16th energy drink brand: 10M+ unique product QR codes, millions of participants and an automated daily draw engine.
- **Deputy Head of IT Department**, Service Agency of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Georgia (Nov 2020 – Apr 2023). Built and led a ~60-person technology organization. Modernized 26+ legacy Delphi and classic ASP applications into .NET microservices, served tens of thousands of citizens daily through two mobile apps, a web portal and APIs, and managed 30+ secure integrations.
- **Senior Software Developer**, TBC Bank (May 2020 – Nov 2020). Moved vendor B6 front-office modules (cash-in/cash-out, customer registration) in-house and rolled the system out from a pilot branch to all branches.
- **Software Solutions Architect**, Greennet (Sep 2019 – May 2020). Led Cisco AppDynamics observability pre-sales and consulting for 10–15 banks, government agencies, public companies and betting operators.
- **Senior Software Developer, Technical Lead**, Softx (Jun 2018 – May 2020). Led 6 developers and coded full-stack (.NET, Angular) on platforms for Global Benefits Georgia, Silknet (silksport.ge) and a multi-tenant SaaS serving 15–20 salons.
- **Android Developer**, GoRealTravel (formerly GoRealEurope) (May 2019 – Apr 2020). Sole Android developer of a travel app covering 85+ cities across Europe and Asia, with offline booking access and Google Play releases.
- **Senior Software Developer**, Treepex (Nov 2017 – May 2018). Built web, B2B and backend platforms for tens of thousands of customers, plus a ranger Android app supporting 100,000+ trees planted.
- **Android Developer**, Tbilisi State University (Sep 2017 – Dec 2017). Sole Android developer of a grant-funded student social and career platform with profiles, job postings and a CV generator.
- **Junior Software Developer**, Ministry of Defence of Georgia (Jun 2017 – Nov 2017). Independently designed, built and deployed Indact, a legal document management system used across the Ministry for tens of thousands of legal acts.
- **Junior Software Developer**, Revenue Service of Georgia (May 2016 – Jul 2016). Built features for the public taxpayer Android app, shipped to production.

#### NuGet packages (88 packages, 91,201 downloads)

- [GM.EntityFramework.Domain](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.EntityFramework.Domain): Domain building blocks — entities, value objects, specifications, results, and repository/unit-of-work abstractions — for DDD-style apps on Entity Framework Core.
- [GM.Exceptions](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.Exceptions): A collection of reusable exception types for .NET apps — a common base exception, HTTP-style exceptions (not found, bad request, already exists, ...), and a FluentValidation-aware ValidationException.
- [GM.FileStorage](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.FileStorage): A provider-agnostic file/blob storage abstraction for .NET: IFileStorageService with streaming Upload/Download, Exists/Delete, presigned URLs, metadata + checksum validation, and a pluggable key/path strategy — with config-driven provider selection.
- [GM.Mediator](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.Mediator): A lightweight, dependency-injection-friendly mediator (send, publish, pipeline behaviors) for .NET.
- [GM.EntityFramework.Persistence](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.EntityFramework.Persistence): EF Core persistence for GM.EntityFramework.Domain — a generic repository, unit of work with transactions, and an auditing DbContext.
- [GM.API.Application](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.API.Application): Application-layer building blocks for the GM.API stack: GM.Mediator wiring plus validation and performance pipeline behaviours, and paged-list models.
- [GM.API.Shared](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.API.Shared): Shared primitives for the GM.API stack, built on GM.Mapper.
- [GM.DistributedLock](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.DistributedLock): A small distributed-lock abstraction for .NET: IDistributedLock with TryAcquire / Acquire (blocking with retry) returning an IAsyncDisposable handle that releases on dispose.

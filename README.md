### Giorgi Metskhvarishvili
**Engineering Leader · Software & Solution Architect** · Cloud-native & distributed systems · .NET · Tbilisi, Georgia

Engineering leader with 13+ years of experience designing and delivering large-scale distributed systems across banking, government, gaming and enterprise domains. I architect platforms from the ground up with Clean Architecture, CQRS, Domain-Driven Design and event-driven principles, and have led cross-functional organizations of up to 60 professionals.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gmetskhvarishvili/) · [NuGet](https://www.nuget.org/profiles/gmetskhvarishvili) · [GitHub](https://github.com/gmetskhvarishvili)

#### Technical expertise

- **Architecture:** Clean Architecture, CQRS, Domain-Driven Design, Event-Driven Design, Microservices, Distributed Systems, Event Storming
- **Languages:** C#, Java, Kotlin, Swift, JavaScript
- **Frameworks:** .NET, ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework, SignalR, Identity Server 4, MediatR, Quartz, Angular, React, Android SDK
- **Data & Messaging:** PostgreSQL, MS SQL, Oracle, Redis, Valkey, Elasticsearch, RabbitMQ, Apache Kafka
- **Cloud & DevOps:** AWS, Microsoft Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, Argo CD, Rancher, OpenShift, Terraform, HashiCorp Vault, Doppler
- **Observability:** Datadog, Dynatrace, Elastic APM, Grafana, Graylog, Cisco AppDynamics

#### Experience

- **Lead Software Architect**, MobileESports (Dec 2022 – present). Architected a global esports platform from the ground up: 35+ microservices, ~80 gaming products, millions of users, 99.98% uptime and ~50 production deployments a week.
- **Software Engineering Team Lead**, Betsson Georgia (Mar 2026 – Jun 2026). Led ~15 engineers across five product teams; drove the modernization roadmap, archived 300+ repositories, decommissioned 50+ services and moved logging to Datadog and secrets to Doppler.
- **Associate Partner, Software Development Chapter Lead**, TBC Bank — Cards & Loyalty (Jul 2024 – Mar 2026). Led backend chapters of 10–20 engineers supporting 10+ product teams across cards, loyalty, subscriptions and government-service integrations.
- **Software Solutions Architect**, Raffle Management Platform (May 2024 – Oct 2025). Designed the end-to-end architecture of a QR-code raffle platform with automated daily draws, rule-based winner selection and notifications.
- **Associate Partner, Software Development Chapter Lead**, TBC Bank — Digital Bank Transformation (Apr 2023 – Sep 2024). Drove the insourcing of transfers, payments and accounts from vendor platforms; recruited 20+ engineers and supported the growth of 50+.
- **Deputy Head of IT Department**, Service Agency of the Ministry of Internal Affairs of Georgia (Nov 2020 – Apr 2023). Led ~60 professionals delivering nationwide platforms for vehicle registration, driving licences and firearms; 30+ integrations; legacy systems re-engineered into microservices.
- **Senior Software Developer**, TBC Bank — Core Banking Modernization (May 2020 – Nov 2020). Built backend capabilities for an in-house replacement of the vendor-provided B6 core banking platform.
- **Software Solutions Architect**, Greennet (Sep 2019 – May 2020). Application performance monitoring and observability consulting with Cisco AppDynamics: assessments, solution design and proposals.
- **Senior Software Developer, Technical Lead**, Softx (Jun 2018 – May 2020). Led delivery of an insurance management platform (Global Benefits Georgia), the silksport.ge media platform, a salon booking platform and a document workflow system.
- **Android Developer**, Go Real Europe (May 2019 – Apr 2020). Sole Android developer of a customer-facing travel management application.
- **Senior Software Developer**, Treepex (Nov 2017 – May 2018). Customer, B2B and admin platforms, backend APIs and a native Android app for forest rangers.
- **Android Developer**, Tbilisi State University — Online Campus (Sep 2017 – Dec 2017). Grant-funded mobile platform connecting students with the university administration.
- **Software Developer Intern**, Ministry of Defence of Georgia (Jun 2017 – Nov 2017). Independently built Indact, a legal document management system adopted by the Legal Department.
- **Software Developer Intern**, Revenue Service of Georgia (May 2016 – Jul 2016). Android application development within the IT Department.

#### Education

- Master of Information Technology Governance and Strategy, Georgian American University (2026 – present)
- Bachelor of Informatics, Ivane Javakhishvili Tbilisi State University (2013 – 2018)

- [GM.Mediator](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.Mediator): A lightweight, dependency-injection-friendly mediator (send, publish, pipeline behaviors) for .NET.
- [GM.EntityFramework.Domain](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.EntityFramework.Domain): Domain building blocks — entities, value objects, specifications, results, and repository/unit-of-work abstractions — for DDD-style apps on Entity Framework Core.
- [GM.EntityFramework.Persistence](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.EntityFramework.Persistence): EF Core persistence for GM.EntityFramework.Domain — a generic repository, unit of work with transactions, and an auditing DbContext.
- [GM.Mapper](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.Mapper): A tiny, dependency-injection-friendly object mapper for .NET, built on Mapster.
- [GM.API](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.API): An opinionated ASP.NET Core Web API starter for .NET.
- [GM.API.Application](https://www.nuget.org/packages/GM.API.Application): Application-layer building blocks for the GM.API stack: GM.Mediator wiring plus validation and performance pipeline behaviours, and paged-list models.
